# Commercial licensing

E2E Platform Forge is publicly viewable for portfolio review and non-commercial, non-production evaluation. It is not open source. Any operational or other commercial use by or for an organization requires a separate written commercial license from LE THANH TUAN.

Commercial use includes production deployment, internal operations, consulting or client delivery, integration into another product, hosted access, redistribution, and paid training or support based on the software.

## Intended engagement model

The usual boundary is:

- **Public core:** remains a separately versioned work owned by LE THANH TUAN.
- **Private adapter:** contains the customer's connections, mappings, configuration, rules, assets, and operational material.
- **Customer data and credentials:** remain outside the public repository and under the controls agreed for the engagement.

A written agreement should define the permitted users and environments, term, support, deployment model, adapter ownership and access, confidentiality, redistribution rights, fees, and exit arrangements. No commercial right is implied by repository access, a demonstration, or a conversation.

To request commercial terms, open a Feedback issue and select **Commercial
licensing inquiry**. Include only non-confidential context; the owner may provide
a private follow-up channel. State:

1. The intended capability and deployment environment.
2. The expected users or systems.
3. Whether a private adapter, implementation work, or support is needed.
4. Any delivery, security, or hosting constraints.

This page explains the intended licensing path and is not legal advice. Final rights and obligations exist only in the signed written agreement; each party should obtain independent advice where appropriate.
